# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define quality assurance strategies, manage testing efforts, and ensure products meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release. They own the quality gates and collaborate with developers and product managers to prevent defects.

### Responsibilities
- Develop QA plans, test strategies, and acceptance criteria
- Lead manual and automated testing efforts
- Report quality metrics and defect trends
- Coordinate with developers on edge cases and quality concerns
- Define Definition of Done (DoD) criteria related to testing
- Participate in release planning to ensure quality readiness

### Goals
- Ensure quality gates are respected and enforced
- Catch regressions and defects early in the development cycle
- Minimize post-release defects and improve customer satisfaction
- Enable fast, confident deployments through comprehensive testing

### How They Interact with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Collaborate on test case design, review code for testability, provide rapid feedback on quality issues
- **With Product Managers**: Clarify acceptance criteria, identify quality trade-offs, validate feature completeness
- **With Project Managers**: Report quality status, identify testing risks and timeline impacts, escalate blockers
- **With Technical Architects**: Review technical design for testability, plan testing strategies for complex systems

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement (define testing requirements)
- Daily standups (report testing progress and blockers)
- PR reviews and code walkthroughs (quality feedback)
- Acceptance testing and release coordination
- Quality metrics dashboards and trend reports

---

## Technical Architect / Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define technical strategy, review architecture decisions, and ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with platform standards. They provide technical guidance and mentor the team on best practices.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct technical design reviews and architecture assessments
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and scalability concerns
- Mentor developers on technical best practices and design patterns
- Document architecture decisions and maintain technical documentation
- Propose and evaluate tooling, frameworks, and infrastructure improvements
- Ensure consistency across codebases and technical standards compliance
- Guide technical trade-offs between speed, maintainability, and performance

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable, scalable, and well-documented solutions
- Reduce technical debt and prevent architectural anti-patterns
- Enable team velocity through sound technical decisions
- Improve system reliability, performance, and observability

### How They Interact with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Provide technical guidance, review code and designs, mentor on best practices
- **With Project Managers**: Identify technical risks and timeline impacts, escalate architectural concerns
- **With Product Managers**: Discuss technical feasibility and trade-offs related to features
- **With QA/Testing Leads**: Design for testability, support test automation strategies

### Typical Communication
- Technical design docs and architecture reviews
- Code review comments and architectural feedback
- Technical risk escalation and decision logs
- Architecture review meetings and design discussions
- Technical knowledge sharing and mentoring sessions

---

## Stakeholder / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Executive Sponsors provide business context, secure resources, remove organizational blockers, and ensure projects align with strategic goals. They represent executive leadership and provide governance oversight.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements, success metrics, and desired outcomes
- Approve project scope, budget, and resource allocation
- Remove organizational barriers and escalate blockers
- Provide executive visibility and status updates to leadership
- Participate in go/no-go decisions and gate reviews
- Ensure alignment with organizational strategy and priorities
- Advocate for the project within the organization

### Goals
- Ensure business value delivery and return on investment
- Secure executive backing and organizational support
- Align projects with strategic initiatives and business goals
- Minimize organizational friction and resource constraints

### How They Interact with Other Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Receive status updates, approve scope changes, escalate organizational blockers
- **With Product Managers**: Validate business requirements and success metrics, approve roadmap priorities
- **With Developers and Technical Leads**: Understand technical constraints and feasibility, make informed trade-off decisions
- **With QA/Testing Leads**: Review quality and release readiness assessments

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder briefings and status updates
- Gate reviews and go/no-go decision meetings
- Budget and resource approval workflows
- Executive escalations and problem resolution
- Strategic alignment reviews

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove process blockers, and help teams continuously improve their delivery practices. They protect the team from scope creep and foster a culture of accountability and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments and process blockers that hinder team progress
- Identify and coach on process improvements and agile practices
- Protect the team from external distractions and unplanned work
- Track and report on team health metrics and retrospective action items
- Coach team members on agile principles and self-organization
- Support team velocity predictability and sustainable pace

### Goals
- Improve team velocity and predictability over time
- Foster psychological safety and continuous learning
- Remove impediments to progress and enable flow
- Build a self-organizing, high-performing team

### How They Interact with Other Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Coordinate ceremonies, escalate process risks and resource constraints
- **With Developers**: Coach on agile practices, facilitate problem-solving and continuous improvement
- **With Product Managers**: Help clarify backlog priorities and acceptance criteria, facilitate planning
- **With QA/Testing Leads**: Ensure testing is integrated into sprint workflows, coordinate quality reviews
- **With Technical Architects**: Facilitate technical decision-making and knowledge sharing

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups (identify blockers and progress)
- Sprint ceremonies (planning, review, retrospective)
- One-on-one coaching and feedback
- Retrospective action item tracking and follow-up
- Process improvement proposals and team health metrics
